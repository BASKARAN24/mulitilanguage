The Solar System consists of the Sun and everything that orbits it, including
eight planets, their moons, dwarf planets, asteroids, and comets. The Sun
contains more than 99 percent of the mass of the entire Solar System. The
four inner planets, Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars, are rocky and small
compared to the outer gas giants. Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar
System, more than twice as massive as all the other planets combined. Saturn
is famous for its spectacular ring system made mostly of ice particles.
Uranus and Neptune are called ice giants because they contain large amounts
of water, ammonia, and methane ice. Earth is the only known planet to
support life, thanks to its liquid water, protective atmosphere, and
moderate temperatures.
